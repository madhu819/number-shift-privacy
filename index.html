Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the NUMBER SHIFT mobile game (the “App”). It is intended to describe the App’s local game features and its use of Google advertising and consent services.

1. Who is responsible for the App?

NUMBER SHIFT is published by its independent developer (“we”, “us” or “our”). For privacy questions, requests or concerns, contact us at the email address below.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores game information locally on your device using the Android WebView’s local storage. This can include game progress, high scores, game history, preferences and other settings needed for the game to work. This information is stored on your device rather than being sent to a NUMBER SHIFT account server. The App does not require you to create an account to play.

You can remove locally stored game information by using the App’s reset option, clearing the App’s storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the App. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove locally saved progress.

3. Advertising and Google services

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information from or about your device when ads are requested, delivered, measured or interacted with. Depending on your device settings, location, consent choices and the applicable Google services configuration, this information may include IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app and interaction information, diagnostic information, approximate location inferred from IP address, and ad delivery or measurement data.

Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy disclosures. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent. The consent message may let you accept, reject or customize choices, depending on the options applicable to you. If a privacy-options control is presented in the App, you can use it to review or change your choices. Your choices may affect whether and which ads can be requested.

You can also review Google’s advertising and privacy controls at My Ad Center and your Android device’s advertising settings.

5. Sharing and recipients

We do not operate a NUMBER SHIFT account service or game-progress server. Information processed for advertising may be received by Google and, as configured in the applicable advertising products and consent choices, advertising partners and service providers involved in ad delivery, fraud prevention, measurement and related services. Their processing is governed by their own privacy terms.

6. Data retention and deletion

Game data stored locally remains on your device until you reset it, clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App. Advertising-related information is handled and retained by Google and its partners under their own policies and retention practices. We do not have access to, or direct control over, information those providers retain.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not designed to request a child’s name, email address or other direct contact details. The person responsible for publishing the App must ensure the App’s target-audience settings, advertising configuration and any child-directed treatment are accurately declared in Google Play and AdMob. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review the concern.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps in the App’s design to limit the information it handles. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Third-party providers are responsible for the security of information they process under their services.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. For information processed by Google or its advertising partners, you may need to exercise those rights directly with the relevant provider. You can contact us using the details below for questions about the App itself.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its advertising services or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when the policy was most recently revised. The current version will be available at this webpage.
